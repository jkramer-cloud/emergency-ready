# RC7 additional review and acceptance items (all pending)

Use fictional records only. Existing review workstation remains the RC3 clinical content review register; this addendum and the Spanish/incident audio registers cover later additions. Do not mark the release approved based on software tests.

- Review guide-opening timestamps and the distinction between tool activity and user-reported observations. Ensure responders cannot mistake a pacing count for actual care.
- Review aggregation across restarts, breath-cue wording, displayed timers and chronological timeline, including missing or delayed observations and the 100-entry retention limit.
- Check whether the handoff panel is helpful and does not distract the active rescuer; verify a helper can open, close, scroll, and show the summary without interrupting CPR or audio capture.
- Check the draft Spanish text for accuracy and plain language. Review all incident audio notices and CPR cues from RC4–RC6.
- On actual Android/iPhone/Chromebook devices verify optional microphone consent, notice audibility and quality, stop and playback, local Save/Delete, hidden-tab/screen lock handling, permission denial, call confirmation and capture termination before actual dialing. Never make real calls in testing.
- Try a synthetic incident, save both text/audio locally, verify downloads and clear the incident. Confirm clearing in-app data does not delete saved files. Close or refresh to verify session-only behavior. Confirm no network transfer of incident data.
- Personal use: confirm recording laws, consent requirements, and safe handling appropriate to the intended location. School use: obtain school authorization separately if required. No real student medical data during preview testing.
